Who do you already know at the company you want?

Type a company or a role. See the people you're already connected to there, and how you know each one. That's who to ask for an introduction.

Your files are read in your browser. Nothing is uploaded or stored.

Your own list in four steps

On a computer: LinkedIn only offers the download there.

  1. Ask LinkedIn for your data

    Me › Settings & Privacy › Data privacy › Download your data. Pick the larger archive.

  2. Download the zip

    LinkedIn emails a link within 24 hours. No need to unzip.

  3. Open it here

    About 20 seconds the first time. Nothing is uploaded.

    Open with my files

  4. Search who you know

    Companies or roles, separated by commas. Download the list as a CSV.

Waiting for LinkedIn's email? Try the sample network meanwhile.

Your data stays with you

  • Read on your computer. Your files are never uploaded, and only the ones the tool needs are opened: your messages stay packed.
  • Nothing kept. No account, no server, no copy. Close the tab and it's gone.
  • Checked on every change. An automated test records every request the page makes and fails if one carries data. The code is open.

Questions

What can't it tell me?

Only people you're connected to: the export doesn't say who they know, so friends of friends can't be listed. No locations either. Company names are matched as people typed them, so a renamed company may need its old name too.

What does the page download?

Its own code from GitHub Pages and a Python runtime (Pyodide) from the jsDelivr CDN, which runs the analysis in your browser. Code comes to you; none of your data goes back.

My zip has no Connections.csv

Request your data again and pick Connections on its own; LinkedIn says that can take up to 48 hours.

Can I use it on my phone?

The sample network, yes. For your own data you need a computer, because LinkedIn only offers the download there.

Is this made by LinkedIn?

No. It's an independent open-source project that reads the files LinkedIn lets you download about yourself.

How Network Circles works

1. Get your files from LinkedIn

  1. On a computer, open LinkedIn and click Me → Settings & Privacy → Data privacy → Download your data.
  2. Choose the larger data archive and click Request archive. The smaller one has no skills, endorsements or posts.
  3. LinkedIn emails you a link within 24 hours; it works for 72 hours. Download the zip. There is no need to unzip it.

2. Open them here

  • Click Open your LinkedIn zip and pick it, or drop it on the page. Already unzipped? Select all the CSV files instead.
  • Everything runs in this browser tab. Nothing is uploaded or stored.

3. Ask who you know

  • Type companies, roles, names, skills or events in the search box, separated by commas. More about it.
  • Click a person to see their circle and what they're linked to. Click a circle to list everyone in it.
  • A circle is a group of people who share an employer or joined your network in the same few days. Generic employers such as "Freelance" don't count.
  • Ways in ranks the companies where you know someone, with who to ask and why. List, Map and Circles are other layouts of the same thing. Layers on the map lines up your skills, organisations, events and groups next to your circles.

How the order works
  1. Worked with you, or endorsed you: they know your work.
  2. Recruiter or senior: they can route an intro.
  3. Met at an event, or added in the last year: they'll remember you.
  4. More people at a company ranks it higher.
